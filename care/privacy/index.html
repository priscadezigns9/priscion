Privacy notice

Private by design.

PRISCION Care is designed to let people ask for support without turning sensitive parts of their lives into public content.

What this covers

This notice explains how the experimental PRISCION Care experience handles wallet identity, case information, supporting documents and contribution records. It is written for the prototype and should be reviewed and expanded with qualified legal advice before any public fundraising operation.

Wallet identity

A wallet is used as the passwordless access key for a private account. PRISCION Care may store the public wallet address, wallet provider, account role, login timestamps and a display nickname. PRISCION Care does not request or store seed phrases, private keys or wallet passwords.

Important: A wallet address is pseudonymous, not automatically anonymous. Cardano transactions may be public and can sometimes be analyzed.

Private case information

Applicants may choose to submit information needed for manual case review, including a hardship story, category, requested amount and supporting documents. Sensitive information such as legal names, addresses, identification numbers, medical details, account numbers and provider records should remain restricted.

Documents are stored in a private storage area. They are not displayed on public case pages by default. Access is limited to authorized PRISCION reviewers and the applicant’s own account where applicable.

What may be public

With the applicant’s consent and after review, a case may display a case number or nickname, avatar, broad region, general need category, privacy-safe story, funding target, progress, public updates and confirmed blockchain transaction references.

Contributions and blockchain records

Contribution transactions are made from a user’s own wallet. A confirmed transaction hash and related on-chain information may be publicly visible and cannot generally be deleted by PRISCION Care. PRISCION Care does not publish an applicant’s complete personal wallet history.

Retention and removal

Off-chain account and case information may be retained while needed to operate, review, secure or document the experiment. An applicant may request correction or removal of off-chain information. Some records may need to remain for security, dispute or audit purposes. On-chain transaction records cannot be removed by the platform.

Service providers

The prototype may use infrastructure providers such as Supabase for database, authentication and private storage services, and Cardano-compatible infrastructure for blockchain interactions. Provider access is limited to what is needed to operate the service.

Children and sensitive submissions

Do not submit information about another person without permission. The prototype is not intended for children to use independently. Applicants should avoid uploading more sensitive information than is necessary for review.

Contact

Privacy questions or removal requests should be directed to the PRISCION contact channel associated with the live project. This experimental notice is not legal advice and may change as the platform develops.
