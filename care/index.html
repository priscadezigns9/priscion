Terms of use

Use it with care.

PRISCION Care is a working experiment in voluntary, wallet-based community support. These terms explain the boundaries of the experience.

Experimental notice: PRISCION Care is not a bank, lender, investment product, money transmitter, registered charity or official financial institution. It does not guarantee funding or a financial outcome.

1. Voluntary participation

You may browse public case information and connect a compatible wallet to create a passwordless account. You are responsible for deciding whether to submit a case or make a contribution.

2. Wallet responsibility

You are responsible for your wallet, recovery method, device security and transaction approvals. PRISCION Care never needs your seed phrase or private key. Do not share those with anyone.

3. Digital-asset contributions

Crypto contributions are voluntary peer-to-peer transfers. Transactions may be irreversible. You should confirm the network, recipient address and amount before signing. PRISCION Care does not promise that a case will reach its target, that funds will be recovered or that a contribution will produce any return.

4. Case submissions

Applicants must provide truthful information and must have permission to submit any document or story involving another person. Do not submit fabricated bills, stolen documents, malicious content or information that is unnecessary for review.

5. Verification

Manual verification is a trust and safety process, not a guarantee, certification or legal finding. PRISCION Care may request clarification, pause a case, reject a case or remove public content when information cannot be verified or when safety requires it.

6. Public case pages

Approved cases may be shown publicly using a nickname, avatar, broad region, general category, privacy-safe story, target and progress. Applicants should understand that any public story, wallet address or blockchain transaction reference may be copied or analyzed by others.

7. Prohibited use

  • Fraud, impersonation or deliberate misrepresentation.
  • Using the platform to launder funds, evade sanctions or facilitate unlawful activity.
  • Harassment, threats, exploitation or attempts to identify applicants without consent.
  • Uploading another person’s private documents without permission.
  • Promoting an investment, loan, token sale or guaranteed financial return through the platform.

8. Experimental availability

The platform may change, pause or become unavailable while it is being developed. Features may be tested on Cardano Preprod before any mainnet contribution flow is enabled. Availability may vary by location and applicable law.

9. No professional advice

PRISCION Care does not provide legal, medical, tax, financial or debt-management advice. Users should seek appropriate professional advice for their own situation.

10. Changes and contact

These terms may be updated as the experiment develops. Continuing to use PRISCION Care after an update means you accept the revised version. Questions should be directed to the PRISCION contact channel associated with the live project.
