Help can move with dignity.

PRISCION Care explores a private, wallet-based way for people to support one another through real hardship — without turning someone’s difficult season into a public spectacle.

A wallet is required for a private account, but it does not need ADA. Public visitors can browse without connecting. Contributions are voluntary and Cardano transactions are irreversible.

The PRISCION promise
Private by design

Applicants use a nickname and wallet-based account. Sensitive evidence is never public by default.

Voluntary by nature

People give freely from their own wallet. PRISCION does not promise a financial outcome.

Transparent where it matters

Confirmed contributions can be checked on Cardano without exposing someone’s entire financial life.
